Supported formats and limits

Static JPG, PNG and WebP are supported. SVG, GIF, HEIC and animated PNG/WebP are not supported. Limits: 20 MB, 24 million pixels and 8,192 px per side. KB means 1,000 bytes; MB means 1,000,000 bytes.

JPG replaces transparent areas with white. PNG uses dimension reduction rather than quality adjustment. Re-encoding may remove metadata such as EXIF. Colors and output may vary by browser. Check the pixel dimensions and format required by your destination.

Very small targets may be unreachable. The tool shows the smallest attempted result without lowering quality or dimensions past its limits. Closing the tab clears your work.
